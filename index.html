Frequently Asked Questions

Practical answers for teams exploring AI and automation for the first time.

What kinds of tasks can you automate?

Customer support responses, lead qualification, data entry, document processing, reporting, scheduling, invoice handling, inventory updates, and the handoffs between your existing tools. If a process is repetitive and rule-based, it is usually a strong candidate.

How long does an automation project take?

A focused automation sprint typically takes four to six weeks from audit to launch. Larger platforms and multi-team rollouts are delivered in phases so you see working results early rather than waiting months for a big release.

Do we need to replace our current software?

Usually not. We build around the systems your team already uses, including CRM, accounting, support, and communication tools. Where a replacement makes sense, we will explain the trade-offs before recommending it.

Is our business data secure?

We follow least-privilege access, encrypted connections, and clear data retention rules. We agree on what data can be used by AI systems, where it is stored, and who can access it before any build begins.

How do we get started?

Book a free automation audit. We will look at one workflow with you, estimate the time and cost involved, and give you a clear recommendation. If we are not the right fit, we will tell you.
