AI Customer Support Assistant

Project information

  • Category: AI Assistants
  • Client: Lumen Finance
  • Timeline: 6 weeks
  • Services: AI chatbot, integration, analytics

Faster answers without losing the human touch

Lumen Finance was handling a growing volume of routine customer questions across email and WhatsApp. Response times were slipping, and agents were repeating the same answers instead of resolving complex cases.

Avero Systems built a support assistant trained on approved product, policy, and onboarding content. It resolves common questions instantly, verifies customer identity where needed, and routes sensitive or complex cases to an agent with a full conversation summary.

What we delivered

  • AI assistant across web chat and WhatsApp
  • CRM and ticketing system integration
  • Human handoff rules and escalation paths
  • Weekly performance and quality reporting

Within the first quarter, routine requests were resolved automatically, first-response time fell below one minute, and the support team could focus on complex customer cases. The assistant is reviewed monthly against real conversations and updated as products change.
