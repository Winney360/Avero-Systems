Careers

Build work that makes work better

Avero Systems is a small, senior team in Nairobi working on practical AI and automation problems for businesses across Africa and beyond. We care about systems that survive contact with real operations, not demos that only work in a presentation.

Who we are looking for

We hire people who can think clearly about a business process, communicate with non-technical teams, and take ownership of outcomes. Our current openings and future roles include:

  • AI Engineers with experience building reliable LLM applications
  • Software Developers who enjoy integrations and internal tools
  • Automation Specialists with a strong operations mindset
  • Data Analysts who can turn messy data into useful decisions

We work in a hybrid setup, value deep focus, and give people real responsibility early. If that sounds like the right environment, send your portfolio and a short note about a process you would like to automate.
